How I work

My production workflow

A practical process for turning a brief into clear, publication-ready copy.

01

Confirm the brief

Clarify audience, objective, format, size, deadline, and required details.

02

Draft the copy

Build a strong headline, concise body copy, and clear call to action.

03

Verify details

Check names, contact information, prices, dates, claims, and terms.

04

Edit for layout

Keep copy within the assigned space and follow brand guidelines.

05

Proofread

Review grammar, spelling, consistency, missing details, and layout.
